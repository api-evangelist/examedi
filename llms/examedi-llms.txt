# Examedi

> Examedi is a Latin American healthtech company providing at-home medical and laboratory testing. Patients book online and a healthcare professional visits their home to collect samples (blood, urine, PCR and more), with results delivered digitally. Examedi operates in Chile and Mexico and is backed by General Catalyst. No public developer program or OpenAPI is published; a live authenticated application backend runs at api.examedi.com.
